Release times, as published

Apple Music, via the public iTunes API, is the exact release clock. Spotify's public artist page and MusicBrainz keep the precision they actually publish, often a year or a full day. A partial date is never upgraded to a fake clock time.

Post time of day

YouTube publishedAt, Instagram embed timestamps, and TikTok createTime are charted by UTC hour and weekday. Official profile links come from MusicBrainz. Handles are not guessed.

Unavailable means unavailable

If a key is missing, a page is login-walled, or a source has no clock time, the dashboard says so. It does not fill the gap with sample listeners or a demo chart. Audience growth is plotted only from exact counts a later crawl actually stored.

Why artists choose this over buried analytics

Chartmetric and similar platforms are excellent for aggregate streams and social counts. They rarely highlight the precise moment content goes live. That timing data is gold for planning future drops, social campaigns, and understanding when your fans are most active.
